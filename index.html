End-to-End Civil Engineering

Complete Home Construction: Groundwork to Handover

Beyond luxury interiors, SSS Interiors undertakes complete turnkey civil construction of independent houses, duplex villas, and residential floors. One trusted team from soil testing to Griha Pravesh.

Certified Structural Engineering

GHMC/HMDA building sanction compliance, soil test load-bearing calculation, earthquake-resistant RCC design, and certified structural vetting.

Fe 550D TMT & M25 Ready-Mix

We use only premier grade materials: Tata Tiscon / JSW Neosteel Fe 550D TMT rebar, UltraTech M20/M25 concrete, and top-tier red bricks/AAC blocks.

Daily Live WhatsApp Updates

Transparent milestone tracking with daily site photos, weekly structural milestone sign-offs, and dedicated on-site civil project engineers.

10-Year Structural Guarantee

Comprehensive warranty covering structural foundation, RCC frame integrity, anti-termite treatment, and multi-layer chemical waterproofing.

Our 8-Stage Civil Execution Process

01
Soil Testing & Approvals

Geotechnical soil investigation, architectural floor planning, 3D elevation render, and HMDA/GHMC permit liaisoning.

02
Excavation & Footing

Machine excavation, chemical anti-termite piping treatment, PCC leveling course, and heavy reinforced column footings.

03
Plinth & RCC Slabs

Plinth beam casting, damp-proof course (DPC), column raising, steel shuttering, and precision vibration roof slab casting.

04
Brickwork & Partitions

Class-A table-molded red clay bricks or AAC blocks laid with polymer mortar for thermal insulation and structural longevity.

05
Concealed MEP Utilities

Finolex/Polycab fire-retardant wiring, modular DB boxes, and Ashirvad/Astral CPVC/UPVC concealed sanitary piping.

06
Plastering & Waterproofing

Double-coat sand-faced external plaster, smooth internal gypsum finish, and Dr. Fixit chemical waterproofing on terraces & wet areas.

07
Flooring & UPVC Glazing

Italian marble or large-format vitrified tiling, teakwood main door frame, and sound-insulated multi-chamber UPVC windows.

08
Turnkey Interior & Handover

Seamless transition to bespoke interior woodwork, false ceilings, luxury lighting, deep sanitization, and celebratory Griha Pravesh handover.

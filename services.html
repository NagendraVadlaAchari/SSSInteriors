Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

✦

Everything you need to know before starting your interior design journey with us.

How long does a typical interior design project take?
A complete 3BHK home interior typically takes 45–60 working days from design sign-off to handover. A single room (like a bedroom or kitchen) can be completed in 15–25 days. These timelines depend on the scope of work, material availability, and site conditions. We provide a detailed project timeline before work begins and adhere to it rigorously.
What is the typical cost of a full home interior in Hyderabad?
Costs vary significantly based on the scope, finishes, and materials chosen. As a rough guide, a 2BHK full interior starts from ₹8–12 lakhs, and a 3BHK from ₹14–25 lakhs. We provide detailed, transparent quotes with no hidden costs after our initial consultation. We work across a range of budgets and always optimise for best value.
Do you provide 3D designs before starting work?
Absolutely! We provide comprehensive 3D photo-realistic renders of every room before any work begins. This allows you to visualise your space, suggest changes, and approve the design with complete confidence. No work starts until you are 100% satisfied with the design.
What warranty do you provide on your work?
We provide a 1-year comprehensive warranty on all our workmanship. For modular kitchens, we additionally pass on the manufacturer's warranty on hardware (Hettich/Häfele provide 10-year warranties on their mechanisms). Our after-sales team is available on WhatsApp for any queries or issues that arise post-completion.
Can I provide my own materials or furniture?
Yes, absolutely. We are flexible and happy to work with client-supplied materials and furniture. Our design team will ensure everything integrates harmoniously. We also offer an extensive materials library and vendor partnerships that allow us to source premium products at competitive prices for you.
Do you work outside Hyderabad?
Our primary service area covers all of Greater Hyderabad including Secunderabad, HITEC City, Gachibowli, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Kondapur, and surrounding areas. We also take select projects in Warangal, Visakhapatnam, and other Telangana/AP cities for large-scale residential and commercial projects.
What payment schedule do you follow?
We follow a transparent milestone-based payment schedule: typically 30% advance to begin design and procurement, 40% at mid-stage execution, and 30% on project completion and handover. We never ask for large lump-sum advances and always document everything in a formal agreement for your protection.

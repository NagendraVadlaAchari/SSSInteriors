Clarity & Confidence

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our turnkey pricing, timelines, material grades, and warranties.

What sets SSS Interiors apart from other Hyderabad interior designers?
Unlike many design agencies who act purely as brokers and sub-contract work to third-party carpentry gangs, SSS Interiors (Sri Sai Sahasra Interiors) owns its mechanized joinery workshop with computerized CNC beam saws, multi-bore machines, and German edge-banders. This gives us 100% control over edge finish, structural durability, and fixed delivery timelines.
What materials and hardware brands do you use?
We operate strictly with certified premium brands: IS 710 Boiling Waterproof (BWP) marine plywood (Century / Greenply), soft-close hardware by Blum (Austria) and Hafele (Germany), Saint-Gobain gypsum boards, Asian Paints Royale zero-VOC PU finishes, and 100% genuine imported Italian marbles.
How long does a full home interior project take to complete?
A standard luxury 3BHK typically requires 45 to 60 working days from the date of final 3D design approval. Palatial 4BHKs and standalone villas take between 75 to 90 working days. We include penalty-backed handover milestone guarantees in our formal service agreement.
Do you provide a formal warranty on your work?
Yes. We provide an unconditional 10-Year Warranty on all modular woodwork and hardware mechanisms against manufacturing defects, borer/termite attacks, and delamination. Furthermore, we provide 1 Year of Complimentary After-Sales Maintenance with periodic doorstep audits.
How does the quotation and payment process work?
Following an initial site audit, we prepare a transparent line-item BOQ (Bill of Quantities) with zero surprise costs. Payments are structured across linked milestones: Booking token (10%), 3D Sign-off & Production commencement (40%), Factory dispatch to site (40%), and Final handover upon your satisfaction (10%).

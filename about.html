Our Story

Crafting Dream Spaces Since 2009

✦

Sri Sai Sahasra Interiors was born from a simple but powerful belief — every family deserves to live in a space that feels both beautiful and uniquely their own. Founded in 2009 in Hyderabad by Mr. Mahesh Kumar Reddy, what started as a small design studio has grown into one of the most trusted interior design brands in Telangana.

Over the past 15+ years, we have transformed more than 500 homes, countless offices, and numerous commercial spaces across Hyderabad, Secunderabad, and beyond. Our philosophy is simple: listen deeply to our clients, design thoughtfully, and execute flawlessly.

We blend timeless Indian aesthetics with contemporary global design trends, creating spaces that honour cultural heritage while embracing modern functionality. From lavish full-home interiors to precision-crafted modular kitchens, every project receives the same degree of dedication and care.

Vision & Mission

✦
🔭

Our Vision

To be the most admired and trusted interior design brand in South India — known for creating spaces that inspire, comfort, and endure. We envision a future where every home in Hyderabad reflects the unique personality and aspirations of the family within it.

We strive to push the boundaries of design innovation while remaining rooted in the warmth and richness of Indian aesthetics — creating homes that are both globally contemporary and deeply personal.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional interior design solutions that exceed client expectations through superior craftsmanship, transparent processes, and unwavering commitment to quality and timely delivery.

We are dedicated to making premium interior design accessible, transparent, and stress-free for every family — listening to your needs, crafting your vision, and transforming your space with pride and passion.
